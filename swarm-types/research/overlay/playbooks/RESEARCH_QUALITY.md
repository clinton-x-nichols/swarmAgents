# Research Quality Gate

Before accepting a research package verify:

1. every material claim has a traceable source or is labeled inference;
2. current questions used current sources where required;
3. primary sources were preferred when available;
4. material conflicting evidence is represented fairly;
5. dates describe both publication time and event/data time when relevant;
6. source limitations and missing evidence are explicit;
7. quotations are exact and not overextended;
8. numbers retain units, denominators, definitions, and time periods;
9. search results were synthesized rather than merely listed;
10. a separate reviewer can reproduce the evidence trail.
