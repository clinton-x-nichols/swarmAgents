# Research swarm commissioning focus

During the Owner interview establish:

- the research question and decision the research must support;
- required freshness and time horizon;
- primary-source requirements and prohibited/weak source classes;
- geographic, market, technical, or regulatory scope;
- deliverable format and expected level of synthesis;
- treatment of conflicting evidence;
- citation and provenance standard;
- whether quantitative analysis or code is required;
- confidentiality and external-search constraints;
- a stopping rule: what constitutes enough evidence.

Role/prompt research should look for current deep-research, evidence-review, literature-review, OSINT (when appropriate and lawful), analyst, critic, and citation-verification agent patterns. Prefer official vendor examples and well-maintained repositories; record provenance and license before adoption.
